transparency & accountability

I believe in transparency. All tips go directly toward:

  • Hosting costs: Server infrastructure, domain registration, and SSL certificates
  • Development time: Building new features, maintaining code, and fixing bugs
  • Content creation: Researching and writing essays, questionnaires, and educational materials
  • Tools & resources: Software licenses, research materials, and professional services

This is an independent, one-person project. There are no corporate sponsors, no venture capital, and no hidden interests. Your support goes directly to maintaining this work.
